# Health Insurance Claims & Premium Analysis

## Project Overview

The **Health Insurance Claims, Premium & Customer Risk Analysis** project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyse health insurance business performance.

The project focuses on understanding customer demographics, insurance policies, premium collections, claim processing, claim settlement performance and medical claim patterns.

The objective is to transform raw insurance data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query – Data Cleaning and Transformation
- DAX – Measures and Calculations
- Excel – Dataset
- Data Modelling – Star Schema

## Dataset Overview

The project uses a healthcare insurance dataset consisting of:

- Dim_Customer
- Dim_Policy
- Dim_Date
- Fact_InsuranceTransactions

The data model follows a Star Schema, with the fact table connected to the dimension tables through one-to-many relationships.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of insurance business performance.

Key analysis:
- Total Customers and Total Policies
- Active Policies
- Total Claims
- Total Premium Collected
- Claim Approval Rate
- Regional Customer Distribution
- Policy Status and Policy Type Distribution

### 2. Claims Analysis

Analyses insurance claim performance and processing activities.

Key analysis:
- Approved, Rejected and Pending Claims
- Claim Approval Rate
- Average Settlement Days
- Claims by Medical Condition
- Monthly and Yearly Claim Trends
- Recent Claim Records

### 3. Premium & Policy Performance

Evaluates premium collections and insurance policy performance.

Key analysis:
- Total Premium Collected
- Average Premium Collected
- Policy Activation Rate
- Premium Payment Success Rate
- Premium Collection Trends
- Policy Type and Policy Status Distribution
- Recent Policy Records

### 4. Customer & Risk Analysis

Examines customer demographics and historical claim activity.

Key analysis:
- Gender Distribution
- Age Group Distribution
- Customer Type Analysis
- Regional Customer Distribution
- Customer Distribution by Occupation
- Claim Amount and Frequency by Age and Gender

### 5. Medical & Hospital Insights

Focuses on medical conditions and their associated claim patterns.

Key analysis:
- Claims by Medical Condition and Claim Status
- Total Claim Amount
- Total Approved Amount
- Average Claim Amount
- Claim Approval Rate
- Medical Condition Ranking
- Medical Condition Claim Analysis
